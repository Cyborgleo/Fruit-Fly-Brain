# Model card — FlyBrain Sandbox

## Current status

The current viewer is a visual prototype with a generated daylight meadow/orchard-edge, a generated male-fly body, first-person spectator movement, and an illustrative walking / short-flight / feeding timeline. It does not load a connectome, simulate neurons, run a biomechanical body model, or sense the environment. The brain overlay and activity trace are decorative illustrations.

## Intended model

The planned project will couple a published adult-fly connectome and a documented neural dynamics model to an existing fly body simulator. MaleCNS v1.0 is the leading dataset candidate because it contains a connected brain and ventral nerve cord from one adult male fly. FlyWire v783 with the published Shiu et al. model is the reference candidate for reproducing previously published sensorimotor results.

These datasets are structural reconstructions. A connectome does not directly provide all synaptic strengths in physiological units, membrane and synaptic dynamics, neuromodulation, learning rules, or a complete account of sensory transduction. Any mapping from anatomy to dynamics or from neural outputs to body controls must be treated as a model choice and documented.

## Intended use

The project is intended for interactive exploration and reproducible educational or research prototypes. A result should not be presented as the behavior or internal experience of the scanned animal without suitable biological validation.

## Planned reporting

Every saved run should identify the connectome release, included neurons and edges, neuron model and parameters, sensory encoders, motor decoder, body model, simulator version, random seed, hardware, and whether it ran live or from a recording.

## Known limitations

- MaleCNS v1.0 raw files are present in the user's workspace but have not been imported into the preview or simulation.
- The procedural body is not a scan-derived or anatomically validated model.
- The preview movement is hand-authored, not generated by a connectome, sensory inputs, or physics.
- The rendered habitat is procedural rather than a photogrammetric or measured location.
- A future connectome-to-body controller may include explicit low-dimensional mappings or existing locomotion controllers; those components must be named and evaluated separately.
- Visual similarity alone does not validate neural or behavioral fidelity.
